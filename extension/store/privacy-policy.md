# Privacy Policy - SemesterHub Browser Extension

**Last Updated:** December 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the SemesterHub browser extension ("Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information. The Extension is designed to help students sync their academic data from Moodle learning management systems to SemesterHub.

## Information We Collect

### Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Extension, we collect the following information from Moodle pages:

1. **Course Information**
   - Course names
   - Course codes (if available)
   - Course URLs

2. **Assignment Information**
   - Assignment titles
   - Due dates
   - Assignment URLs
   - Assignment types (assignment, quiz, forum, etc.)

3. **Authentication Information**
   - Your SemesterHub user ID (stored locally)
   - Authentication tokens (stored locally, expires after 30 days)

### Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do NOT collect:

- Moodle passwords or credentials
- Grades or academic performance data
- Course content (lectures, materials, submissions)
- Personal messages or forum posts
- Financial information
- Location data
- Browsing history outside of Moodle pages

## How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

1. Display your courses and assignments in SemesterHub
2. Track assignment due dates and send reminders
3. Provide a unified academic dashboard

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** Authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- **Server Storage:** Course and assignment data is stored in SemesterHub's secure servers
- **Data Association:** All data is associated with your SemesterHub account and is not shared with other users

## Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with:

- Third parties
- Advertising networks
- Analytics providers
- Other users

Your academic data is private and visible only to you.

## Data Security

We implement security measures including:

- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- JWT-based authentication
- Secure token storage
- Server-side validation of all requests

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access:** View all data associated with your account
2. **Deletion:** Delete your SemesterHub account and all associated data
3. **Portability:** Export your data from SemesterHub

## Data Retention

- Course and assignment data is retained as long as your account is active
- Deleted accounts have all associated data removed within 30 days
- Authentication tokens expire after 30 days

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the Extension or via email.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

- Email: support@semesterhub.com
- Website: https://semester-dash.vercel.app

## Consent

By using the SemesterHub browser extension, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.
