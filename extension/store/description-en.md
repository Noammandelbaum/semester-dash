# SemesterHub - Moodle Sync

Sync your courses and assignments from Moodle directly to SemesterHub.

## Key Features

- Automatic sync of courses and assignments from Moodle
- Support for all educational institutions
- Full Hebrew support with RTL layout
- Complete privacy - your data only
- Automatic due date detection

## Supported Institutions

- Tel Aviv University
- Hebrew University of Jerusalem
- Technion
- Ben-Gurion University
- Bar-Ilan University
- University of Haifa
- Jerusalem College of Technology (JCT)
- Afeka College
- Shenkar College
- And many more...

The extension supports ANY Moodle system - even if your institution isn't listed!

## How It Works

1. Install the extension
2. Log in to your SemesterHub account
3. Navigate to your institution's Moodle page
4. Click the sync button

Your courses and assignments will immediately appear in your SemesterHub dashboard!

## Requirements

- SemesterHub account (free)
- Access to your institution's Moodle system

## Privacy

The extension only collects the following information:
- Course names
- Assignment titles and due dates
- Course and assignment URLs

We do NOT collect:
- Passwords
- Grades
- Course content
- Personal information beyond name and email

All data is stored in your SemesterHub account only.
